### 1. How much does a business website cost?

Our four-page business website starts at 80,000 AMD, or 40,000 AMD when you submit a project request through our form. The package includes Home, Services, About, and Contact pages. The final quote depends on your agreed design, content, and functionality.

Complete the project request form and select the options you need. You receive 50% off the listed one-time website services, plus free Armenian, English, and Russian language setup. Translation and content preparation are agreed separately. Monthly maintenance and third-party charges are excluded. Submitting the form requests a quote; it does not require payment.

### 3. Will I have to pay monthly for my website?

Website development is a one-time project fee for the agreed scope. Optional maintenance costs 15,000 AMD per month and is separate from development. Your chosen domain, hosting, and third-party tools may have their own recurring charges, which we clarify before starting.

### 4. How long will it take, and what do I need to provide?

We agree on the delivery schedule after reviewing your project. To get started, share a description of your business, your services, contact details, and any logo, photos, or text you already have. If something is missing, tell us so we can discuss what help you need.

### 5. Do I need a website if I already have Instagram?

A website complements Instagram by giving customers one clear place to explore your services, learn about your business, and contact you. It can connect to your existing booking tools and social profiles, while also giving your business opportunities to appear in search results.

[Read the website and Instagram guide ↗](/en/blog/website-and-instagram/)

### 6. Will my website work on phones and appear on Google?

We create responsive layouts for phones, tablets, and computers, and include basic technical SEO such as page titles, descriptions, clear structure, and a sitemap. Google decides whether and when to index pages. Search rankings depend on content, competition, and other factors, so specific positions are not guaranteed.

### 7. Can I add more pages, a blog, or an online store?

Yes. Under the form offer, additional pages beyond the four-page package start at 5,000 AMD each, blog setup at 10,000 AMD, and product/catalog setup at 15,000 AMD. Product quantities, payment integrations, shipping, content-management tools, and article writing are scoped separately. These starting prices apply when included in your promotional quote; later additions are quoted separately.

### 8. What happens after I submit a project request?

We review your request and contact you within one hour using your preferred contact method to discuss the project and prepare a quote. You do not need to pay to submit the form, and submission does not commit you to purchasing a website.
