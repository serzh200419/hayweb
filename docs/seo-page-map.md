# HayWeb SEO page map

Reviewed 2026-10-14. This is a content-intent map, not a keyword-volume forecast. Titles, descriptions, H1s and listed links below were extracted from the current production-output HTML. Search phrases and intent assignments are **editorial suggestions**, not verified Search Console queries. There are 28 canonical pages plus a noindex 404 page.

## Qualitative wording research

Public search results were inspected for Armenian, English and Russian wording. Observed commercial-language examples: [Agile Business](https://agile-business-pro.com/hy) and [Creative Web](https://creativeweb.am/) use Armenian wording such as «կայքերի պատրաստում»; [Arising Media](https://www.arisingmedia.us/services/business-website-design/) uses “business website design”; [Business Site](https://www.site2b.ua/) uses «создание сайтов для бизнеса». These observations establish phrasing in use, not search frequency, demand, quality of those providers or HayWeb’s likely rankings. Related phrases and page-level assignments below are editorial choices. No competitor copy, service promises or prices were reused.

## Architecture decision

The homepage introduces the complete business offer. Services helps readers compare scope and prices. The dedicated business-website page covers material preparation, page choices, deliverables and updates. The multilingual page covers equivalent customer journeys, translation responsibilities and maintaining language versions. These two topics are substantial and relevant to the worldwide audience, particularly Armenian owners in Armenia and abroad. They use stable IDs and /[lang]/services/[topic]/ URLs, without city/country or industry doorway pages. Redesign remains in the overview: a useful dedicated treatment needs more agreed migration/audit/support scope than the current offer specifies. No new articles were added.

## Root and indexing policy

The Armenian root entry is a concise brand/language gateway, not a duplicate landing page. The three homepages point x-default to /. Inner pages point x-default to their equivalent Armenian URL. Article relationships preserve their real translated slugs. /404.html is a shared branded recovery page with Armenian, English and Russian text; it is noindex, excluded from sitemap and not a search landing page, so it has no target search phrase. Local review URLs remain localhost until the actual public origin is supplied; hosted guards require the confirmed production origin.
